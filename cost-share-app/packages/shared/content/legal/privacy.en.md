## 2. What we collect
- **Account data:** email, display name, login identifiers (e.g. Apple/Google sign-in) [verify which].
- **Group and expense data:** groups, members' names as entered by users, expenses, amounts, currencies, notes. This can include data about people who entered it about you.
- **Purchase data:** subscription and Trip Pass status, transaction IDs, store, country (we never receive card details).
- **Usage and device data:** app interactions, device model, OS, app version, anonymous app user ID, IP-based approximate location.
- **Crash and diagnostic data:** error reports and performance data.
- **Advertising data (free version):** device advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID, only where permitted), ad interactions.
We don't ask for sensitive data. Please don't enter it in notes.

For Israeli data, transfers follow the Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761-2001 [verify]. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

## 6. Ads
The free version shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob.
- **iOS:** we only use the advertising identifier if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you get non-personalized ads.
- **EU/EEA/UK:** before ads load, we ask for your consent through Google's consent tool (UMP). You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy [verify the menu path].
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings.
Pro users don't see ads.

- Crash and analytics data: up to [90 days / 12 months — verify provider settings].

## 8. Deleting your account
Settings → Delete account. We delete your account and personal data, except shared expenses (anonymized) and records we must keep by law. Deleting the app or account doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel in store settings.
