# ASO keyword research (7 locales)

Research method: Google Trends, Apple Search Ads suggestion UI (no spend), Play Console search terms (when available), and competitor SERP checks for **Splitwise**, **Tricount**, **Settle Up**, and generic intents (*split bills*, *trip expenses*, *roommate expenses*).

## Shared intent clusters

| Cluster | Example EN | Localized focus |
|---------|------------|-----------------|
| Core split | split bills, group expenses | Trip + roommate variants per locale |
| Travel | trip expenses, travel budget | Holiday / vacation wording (FR, ES, DE) |
| Competitor alt | Splitwise alternative | “Alternative” phrasing where store policy allows |
| Settle-up | settle debts, who owes whom | Bit/PayBox (HE), multi-currency (RU, EU) |
| RTL / locale | Hebrew RTL, Arabic | Trust + native UI in HE/AR listings |

## Locale notes

- **en:** Primary keywords: split bills, trip expenses, group expenses, Splitwise alternative, settle up.
- **he:** חלוקת הוצאות, חשבון משותף, טיול, שותפים לדירה, Bit, PayBox.
- **ru:** делить расходы, поездка, долги, упрощение переводов.
- **ar:** تقسيم الفاتورة, مصاريف الرحلة, حساب مشترك, RTL.
- **fr:** partage des dépenses, voyage, colocation, note de frais.
- **es:** dividir gastos, viaje, piso compartido, gastos compartidos.
- **de:** kosten teilen, reise, wg, ausgaben aufteilen.
